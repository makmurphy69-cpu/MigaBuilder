Media Convert

Popular media conversions, handled in your browser.

🔒 Media stays on this device. Long or high-resolution files can take time. (Live transcription tells you first if your browser can only do it online.)

Video to GIF

Extract audio from video

The browser records the file's audio track into a WebM audio file. Keep this tab open until playback finishes.

Transcribe a file into text and subtitles

🔒 Speech recognition (OpenAI's Whisper) runs on this device, so the recording is never uploaded. The model downloads once and your browser keeps it for next time.

SRT and VTT subtitle files work in YouTube, VLC, video editors and most players. Corrections you make in the transcript are used when the lines still match the timed segments.

Live speech transcription

This uses your browser's speech recognition. Play a recording nearby or speak into the microphone. Availability and language support depend on the browser.

When your browser can recognise speech on this device, your voice stays here. Otherwise it is sent to the browser maker's speech service (Google in Chrome), and you are asked first.

For fully private transcription of a recording, use Transcribe a file →

Compress, trim and convert

🔒 Runs locally with FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly; your video never leaves this tab. The engine (about 31 MB) downloads once on first use. Large videos can be slow on phones.

Removes GPS location, camera and phone model, recording date and other tags without re-encoding, so quality is unchanged and it is fast. Trimming still works; the other settings are not used.

GIFs are capped at 20 seconds and 640 px wide so they stay a sensible size.

About Media Convert

Media Convert compresses, trims, rotates and resizes video, makes GIFs and MP3s, removes location data, extracts audio and transcribes speech.

How to use Media Convert

  1. Choose a video or audio file and pick GIF, audio extraction, live transcription, or compress, trim and convert.
  2. Press “Create and download GIF”.
  3. Check the result, then download it or copy the transcript.

Can I make a GIF from a video?

Yes. Choose the video, pick the part you want, and create and download the GIF.

Is Media Convert free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
