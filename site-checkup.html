About Website Checkup

Website Checkup reviews HTML for common SEO, accessibility and mobile problems and explains how to fix them.

How to use Website Checkup

  1. Upload or paste the HTML you want to review.
  2. Press “Run website checkup”.
  3. Work through the SEO and accessibility fixes, then download the report.

What does it check?

Common SEO, accessibility and mobile problems such as page titles, image descriptions and mobile settings. Download the report when you are done.

Is Website Checkup free?

Yes. Website Checkup is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Website Checkup runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
