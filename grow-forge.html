About Grow Forge

Grow Forge is a free gardening guide for growing food and plants at home — on a windowsill, a balcony or in a garden — or out in nature. It covers 46 vegetables, fruits, herbs, flowers, houseplants, mushrooms, trees and wildflower meadows, each with a step-by-step method, what equipment helps and how to manage without it, care, common problems and fixes, when and how to harvest, and how the plant lives in the wild.

How to use Grow Forge

  1. Search for a plant or choose one from the list; filter by where you grow and how easy it is.
  2. Pick your climate to see the right sowing and harvest months, then read the step-by-step guide, the equipment and no-equipment options, care and problems.
  3. Add plants to My garden to see when they should sprout and be ready, and download your grow plan.

Which plants are easiest for beginners?

Radishes, salad leaves, microgreens, bush beans, potatoes in a bag, courgettes, mint, chives and strawberries. Use the Plant finder to get suggestions for your space, light and time.

Can I grow food without buying equipment?

Yes. Every guide has a “Without equipment” list: sunny windowsills, recycled containers, compost from kitchen scraps, rain water, mulch, seed saving, cuttings and methods like the Three Sisters and the Kratky jar.

Is Grow Forge free and private?

Yes. It is free with no account. All the guides are built into the page and your garden plan is kept only in your browser.
