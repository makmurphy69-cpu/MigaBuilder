Answer key

  1. C) Reactive non-metals — Nitrogen: Reactive non-metals — Gases or brittle solids that do not conduct well. They make most of living things (C, H, N, O, P, S) and the halogens.
  2. C) Phosphorus — Bones & teeth is made from Calcium, Phosphorus, Oxygen, Hydrogen, Carbon, Nitrogen. Your body builds a mineral–protein composite: the mineral is hard, the collagen stops it shattering.
  3. A) Solid — Lithium melts at 180.5 °C and boils at 1,329.8 °C.
  4. A) Chlorine — PVC is made from Carbon, Hydrogen, Chlorine. Vinyl chloride molecules are linked into chains.
  5. A) Argon — Ar is Argon, atomic number 18.
  6. D) Helium — MRI magnet is made from Niobium, Titanium, Helium, Copper. Cooled to −269 °C, the wire loses all electrical resistance and makes a very strong magnetic field.
  7. D) Boron — Boron: Heat-resistant borosilicate glass (lab glass, oven dishes); fibreglass insulation; detergents; strong neodymium magnets (Nd₂Fe₁₄B); control rods in nuclear reactors; plant fertiliser.
  8. B) Neon — Ne is Neon, atomic number 10.
  9. C) Solid — Sodium melts at 97.8 °C and boils at 882.9 °C.
  10. B) Reactive non-metals — Carbon: Reactive non-metals — Gases or brittle solids that do not conduct well. They make most of living things (C, H, N, O, P, S) and the halogens.
