Answer key

  1. A) Uranus — Because of its tilt, each pole gets about 42 years of sunlight followed by 42 years of darkness.
  2. A) Moon — Moon: The Moon drifts 3.8 cm farther from Earth every year — measured with lasers bounced off mirrors left by Apollo astronauts.
  3. D) Saturn — Saturn has 274 known moons (2025), far more than Jupiter.
  4. D) Pluto — Surface gravity: Pluto 0.6 m/s², Mercury 3.7 m/s², Jupiter 23.1 m/s², Uranus 8.7 m/s².
  5. D) Mars — Olympus Mons is about 22 km high — roughly two and a half times the height of Mount Everest.
  6. B) About 8 minutes — 150 million km ÷ 300,000 km/s ≈ 500 s = 8 min 20 s.
  7. D) Mercury — Mercury 167 °C, Uranus -195 °C, Jupiter -110 °C, Earth 15 °C. Venus is hotter than Mercury because of its thick greenhouse atmosphere.
  8. A) It has not cleared its orbit of other objects — In 2006 the IAU said a planet must have cleared its orbital neighbourhood; Pluto shares the Kuiper Belt with many similar bodies.
  9. C) Uranus — Uranus’s axis is tilted 98°.
  10. B) Neptune — Neptune: Neptune takes 165 years to orbit the Sun — it completed its first orbit since its discovery in 2011.
