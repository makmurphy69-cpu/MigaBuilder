Answer key

  1. B) 1981 — Surgery on babies before birth: 1981, San Francisco, USA. Operating on a baby still in the womb to fix problems that would cause serious harm before birth.
  2. D) Edwin Smith Papyrus — Edwin Smith Papyrus (c. 1600 BCE (copy of older texts)) → Squint surgery (1839) → Reattaching severed limbs (1962) → LASIK laser eye surgery (1990).
  3. D) Glaucoma surgery — Glaucoma surgery (1856): Making a new drainage path for fluid in the eye to lower the pressure that damages the optic nerve.
  4. D) Breast implants and reconstruction — Breast implants and reconstruction (1962): Silicone implants or the patient’s own tissue are used to rebuild a breast after cancer, or to change its size.
  5. D) Rickman Godlee; later Harvey Cushing — Brain tumour removal (craniotomy) (1884): Rickman Godlee; later Harvey Cushing.
  6. B) 16 October 1846 — Ether anaesthesia: 16 October 1846, Boston, USA. The first public operation under ether, at Massachusetts General Hospital, showed that surgery could be painless.
  7. D) 1954 — Kidney transplant: 1954, Boston, USA. Placing a healthy kidney from a donor into a person whose kidneys have failed.
  8. D) Theodor Billroth — Stomach removal (gastrectomy) (1881): Theodor Billroth.
  9. D) Open-heart surgery with a heart-lung machine — Open-heart surgery with a heart-lung machine — 1953, Philadelphia, USA.
  10. B) Vasectomy — A “no-scalpel” method was developed in China in 1974.
