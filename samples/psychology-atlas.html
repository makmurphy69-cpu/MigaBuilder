Answer key

  1. B) 1950s — Behaviour therapy is usually dated from 1950s. Mary Cover Jones removes a boy’s fear of rabbits by gradual exposure (1924).
  2. D) Family systems therapy — Family systems therapy: Family therapists see problems as part of the patterns in a whole family or relationship, not only inside one person. Changing how family members communicate and relate can help the person with symptoms.
  3. A) Developmental psychology — Jean Piaget — Stages of cognitive development (key work: The Origins of Intelligence in Children).
  4. B) 1970s — Cultural & cross-cultural psychology is usually dated from 1970s. The International Association for Cross-Cultural Psychology is founded (1972).
  5. A) Structuralism (Wundt & Titchener) — Wilhelm Wundt — Founded experimental psychology; voluntarism; Völkerpsychologie (key work: Principles of Physiological Psychology).
  6. D) Biological psychology & neuropsychology — Alexander Luria — Founded neuropsychological assessment (key work: The Working Brain).
  7. B) Andy Clark — Andy Clark (b. 1957), a key figure of Predictive processing & the Bayesian brain: Predictive processing and the embodied mind.
  8. D) Philippe Pinel — Philippe Pinel (1745–1826), a key figure of Moral treatment & asylum reform: Humane treatment and careful clinical observation.
  9. C) 1947 — This happened in 1947, in the history of Personality psychology.
  10. B) Cognitive psychology — Ulric Neisser — Named and defined cognitive psychology (key work: Cognitive Psychology).
