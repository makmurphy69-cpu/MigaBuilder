Answer key

  1. D) Flush toilet — Flush toilet (1596): A toilet that washes waste away with water.
  2. B) 1907 — Plastic (Bakelite): 1907, New York, USA. The first fully synthetic plastic, made from chemicals rather than natural materials.
  3. A) Modern electric car — Electric cars were popular in 1900, before cheap petrol and Ford’s Model T.
  4. C) Power station and grid — Edison’s Pearl Street station first powered about 400 lamps.
  5. A) Plough — Plough (c. 4000 BCE) → Photography (1826) → Motion pictures (1895) → QR code (1994).
  6. A) Crane — Crane (c. 515 BCE): A machine with pulleys and a winch to lift heavy stones.
  7. C) c. 3500 BCE — Wheel: c. 3500 BCE, Mesopotamia and Eastern Europe. A round disc turning on an axle. Potters used it before carts.
  8. A) Jet engine — Jet engine — 1937, England and Germany.
  9. D) Lighthouse — Lighthouse — c. 280 BCE, Alexandria, Egypt.
  10. C) Steam locomotive — Steam locomotive (1804; Rocket 1829): A steam engine on wheels that pulls trains along rails.
