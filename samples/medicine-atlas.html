Answer key

  1. D) 1981 — Ivermectin: 1981, Japan and USA. A drug from soil bacteria that treats river blindness and other parasitic worms.
  2. B) 1817 — Cholera pandemics: 1817, From Bengal across the world. A bacterial infection of the gut that causes severe diarrhoea and can kill within hours.
  3. C) 2021 — Malaria vaccines: 2021, Africa. The first vaccines against a parasite, recommended for children in Africa.
  4. D) Morphine — Morphine — 1804, Germany.
  5. D) Antonine Plague — Antonine Plague (165 CE) → Handwashing (Semmelweis) (1847) → MRI scanner (1977) → COVID-19 pandemic (2019).
  6. D) John Snow and the Broad Street pump — A replica of the pump without its handle stands in Broadwick Street, London.
  7. D) Florence Nightingale and modern nursing — Florence Nightingale and modern nursing (1854) → Spanish flu (1918) → Streptomycin (1943) → Oral rehydration solution (1968–1971).
  8. B) Structure of DNA — Franklin died in 1958 and could not share the 1962 Nobel Prize.
  9. C) Rabies vaccine — Rabies vaccine (1885): Pasteur treated 9-year-old Joseph Meister, bitten by a rabid dog, with a weakened virus. He survived.
  10. B) Plague of Athens — Plague of Athens (430 BCE): An epidemic that killed perhaps a quarter of Athens during its war with Sparta, including the leader Pericles.
