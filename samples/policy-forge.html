Privacy policy for Sunrise Bakery

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Sunrise Bakery AB ("we", "us") collects when you use Sunrise Bakery (https://sunrisebakery.com), why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what rights you have. We only collect what we need, and we never sell your personal data for money; see "Advertising" below for how advertising partners receive data.

Who is responsible for your data

Sunrise Bakery AB is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact us about privacy at privacy@sunrisebakery.com.

Children

Sunrise Bakery is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Who we share data with

We use these service providers. They handle data on our behalf under a data processing agreement, or as independent controllers where stated in their own privacy policies (for example for advertising and embedded content):

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting the website (USA)
  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC — Google Analytics: to measure visits and how pages are used (USA)
  • Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. — Meta (Facebook) Pixel: to measure and target advertising (Ireland / USA)
  • Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. / Stripe, Inc. — Stripe: to take payments and prevent fraud (Ireland / USA)
  • the form provider — Form service (Formspree, Netlify Forms, Google Forms): to receive messages sent through forms (USA)
  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC — YouTube videos: to show embedded videos (USA)
  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC — Google Maps: to show a map (USA)
  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC — Google Fonts (loaded from Google): to show the website’s fonts (USA)

We may also share data when the law requires it, to protect our rights, or with a buyer if the business is sold — in that case this policy continues to apply.

Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some providers (Cloudflare, Google Analytics, Meta (Facebook) Pixel, Stripe, Form service (Formspree, Netlify Forms, Google Forms), YouTube videos, Google Maps, Google Fonts (loaded from Google)) may process data in countries outside the EU/EEA, mainly the USA. We only use such providers when the transfer is protected: the provider is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, or we have signed the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses with extra safeguards where needed. Contact us for a copy of these safeguards.

Cookies and similar technology

We use cookies. Read our cookie policy for the full list and how to change your choice.

How we protect your data

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), limit who can access personal data, use strong passwords and two-factor log-in for our accounts, and choose providers with good security. No system is perfectly secure; if a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access)
  • have wrong data corrected
  • have your data deleted ("right to be forgotten")
  • limit how we use your data
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, and always to direct marketing
  • get your data in a common, machine-readable format (portability)
  • withdraw consent at any time — this does not affect what we did before
  • complain to a supervisory authority: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY) (https://www.imy.se)

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose; to delete it; to correct it; to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising; to limit the use of sensitive personal information; and not to be treated differently for using these rights. Our advertising cookies may count as "sharing" under California law. Use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link on our site or turn off advertising cookies; we also honour the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser. In the last 12 months we collected the categories described in "What we collect and why" (identifiers, internet activity, commercial information where you bought something). You or an authorised agent can make a request at privacy@sunrisebakery.com. We will verify your request and answer within 45 days.

To use your rights, email privacy@sunrisebakery.com. We answer within one month (45 days for California requests) and may need to confirm who you are first. It is free.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we update this page and the date at the top. If the change is important, we will tell you on the site or by email.
