Answer key

  1. D) Crossbow — Crossbow (by c. 500 BCE) → Naginata (Heian period (12th century)) → Percussion lock (1807) → Aircraft carrier (1918).
  2. A) Hand axe — Hand axe (c. 1.76 million years ago) → Wootz and Damascus steel (c. 300 BCE) → Medieval crossbow (c. 10th century (Europe)) → Missile defence (Iron Dome) (2011).
  3. B) Japanese sword (tachi and katana) — Japanese sword (tachi and katana) — c. 10th century, Japan.
  4. C) c. 23,000 years ago — Throwing stick and boomerang: c. 23,000 years ago, Poland (oldest find) and Australia. A curved, flat stick thrown spinning at birds and small animals. Only light returning boomerangs come back; heavy hunting ones fly straight.
  5. D) Assyrian, Greek and Roman engineers — Siege tower (c. 860 BCE): Assyrian, Greek and Roman engineers.
  6. B) c. 4th century BCE — Chain mail: c. 4th century BCE, Celtic Europe. A shirt of thousands of small iron rings linked together. Flexible, strong against cuts, and used for almost 2,000 years.
  7. A) Light field artillery — Light field artillery — 1630s, Sweden.
  8. D) Iron sword — Iron sword (c. 1200 BCE) → Gastraphetes (belly bow) (c. 400 BCE) → Lever-action repeating rifle (1860) → Armed drone (2001).
  9. D) Battering ram — Battering ram (c. 870 BCE) → Mortar (15th century) → Musket (1520s) → Nuclear submarine (1954).
  10. D) Erlitou and Shang dynasty soldiers — Dagger-axe (ge) (c. 1900 BCE): Erlitou and Shang dynasty soldiers.
