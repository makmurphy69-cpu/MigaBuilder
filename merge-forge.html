Turning a PowerPoint into a video?
Click here first — one quick export step
  1. Open your presentation in PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides.
  2. Use File → Export → Images (PowerPoint/Keynote) or File → Download → PNG image (current slide) repeated per slide, or File → Download → JPEG zipped in Google Slides — every tool has a one-click "export slides as images" option.
  3. Upload the resulting image files below, in slide order.

Browsers can't open or render a raw .pptx file directly — there's no PowerPoint engine built into a webpage — so this one export step stands in for that. Everything after this is automatic.

About Merge Forge

Merge Forge joins slide images, video clips, music and text overlays into one video, with smooth audio fades and a live preview.

How to use Merge Forge

  1. Add clips, images, text, and music in the order you want.
  2. Press “Merge media”.
  3. Preview the timeline and export the combined video.

Can I combine clips from other MigaBuilder tools?

Yes. Add videos made in Video Forge, Record Forge or Clip Forge together with images and music, put them in order and export one video.

Is Merge Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
