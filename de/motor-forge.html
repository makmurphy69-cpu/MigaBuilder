How well it works

    How Motor Forge works

    Engine: the size (displacement) comes from the bore, stroke and number of cylinders. Torque is the size times a typical cylinder pressure (BMEP) for that kind of engine — about 12–13 bar for a petrol engine without a turbo, and roughly double with boost. Higher compression burns the fuel more efficiently. The cam profile shapes where the engine breathes best, and friction grows with piston speed. Power is torque × revs.

    Performance: a simulation steps through a full-throttle run a hundredth of a second at a time. At each moment it picks the best gear, works out the pushing force at the wheels, limits it by tyre grip (with weight moving backwards as you accelerate, and wheelies on a motorcycle), and subtracts air drag (½·ρ·Cd·A·v²) and rolling resistance. Braking is limited by grip, and on a motorcycle by the rear wheel lifting. Fuel use comes from the power needed at a steady 100 km/h and how efficient the engine is at that light load.

    These are estimates for learning and comparing designs, usually within 10–20% of real vehicles. Real cars and motorcycles also need crash safety, cooling, emissions and road approval.

    About Motor Forge

    Motor Forge is a car and motorcycle design tool: start from a hatchback, sports car, SUV, pickup, electric car, scooter, sport bike, cruiser or dirt bike, build its engine and see how well it would work.

    How to use Motor Forge

    1. Pick Car or Motorcycle and a starting vehicle, then change the engine: cylinders, bore, stroke, compression, revs and turbo.
    2. Open the “Engine” tab to see the power and torque curve.
    3. Change the weight, tyres, gearbox and aero, run the 0–100 test, read the results and checks, and pin designs to compare them.

    Can I design an electric car or motorbike?

    Yes. Choose Electric motor, then set the motor power and battery size. You get acceleration, top speed, energy use and range.

    Is Motor Forge free?

    Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. It runs entirely in your browser, so your designs stay on your device.
