About Screen Share Forge

Screen Share Forge gives live remote help: send a link, get a code, watch the other person's screen and chat while you help.

How to use Screen Share Forge

  1. Create a support session and choose what you want to share.
  2. Press “Start session”.
  3. Share the link safely and end the session when help is finished.

Does the other person need to install anything?

No. Both of you only need a web browser; you share a link and a code.

Is Screen Share Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

The screen and chat travel directly between the two browsers. Nothing is installed and nothing is recorded.

Part of MigaBuilder. Once connected, video, audio and chat travel directly between the two browsers (peer-to-peer) — this tool's server only relays the one-time connection handshake and never sees or stores the call itself. Screen sharing always goes through your browser's own picker, and you (or they) can stop it at any time. This needs a one-time setup by the site owner — see cloudflare-worker/README.md.
