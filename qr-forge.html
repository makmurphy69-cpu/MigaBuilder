About QR Forge

QR Forge turns a link, text, Wi-Fi network, contact card, phone number, email, text message, map location or calendar event into a QR code. Choose the colours, dot and corner shapes, add your logo and a line of text such as “Scan me”, and download it as PNG or SVG. Every code has the blank border scanners need.

How to use QR Forge

  1. Paste the destination link or text and choose the QR style.
  2. Press “Create QR code”.
  3. Scan-test it and download the final code.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. The QR code holds your link or text directly, so it keeps working for as long as the link does.

Is QR Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
