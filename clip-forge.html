🪞 Talk to yourself: two takes, one frameFilm yourself on one side of the sofa talking, then on the other side answering. Clip Forge joins the two takes so there are two of you.
  1. Put the camera on a tripod or prop it up. Don't move it between takes, and keep the light the same.
  2. Take 1: sit on one side and say your line, then stay still and "listen" for as long as the answer will take.
  3. Take 2: sit on the other side, wait, then answer. Don't cross the middle of the picture.
  4. Making a TikTok, Reel or Short? Film upright, so you both fit in a tall picture.

One-time setup: get a Google OAuth Client ID (click to expand)
  1. Go to the Google Cloud Console, create (or pick) a project, and enable the YouTube Data API v3.
  2. Under APIs & Services → OAuth consent screen, set it up as "External", add the youtube.upload scope, and add your own Google account under "Test users" (this avoids needing Google's full app-verification review, since only you will use it).
  3. Under APIs & Services → Credentials, create an OAuth client ID of type "Web application". Add the exact URL this page is running at (e.g. https://your-username.github.io) under "Authorized JavaScript origins".
  4. Copy the generated Client ID (it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com) into the field above.
  5. Click "Connect Google account" and approve access — Google will warn that the app is unverified, which is expected for a personal project; click through as the test user you added.

If any step here fails or your browser blocks the upload, you can always fall back to downloading the video file above and uploading it manually at studio.youtube.com.
