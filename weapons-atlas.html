Weapons Atlas

The history of weapons, from the first stone tools to drones: who invented each one, how it worked, how it was used and what it changed. Browse the cards or the timeline, then play the quiz.

About Weapons Atlas

Weapons Atlas tells the history of weapons and armour in 160 illustrated entries across nine eras: the Stone Age, the Bronze Age, the Iron Age and classical world, the Middle Ages, the age of gunpowder, the industrial age, the World Wars, the Cold War and today. It covers swords and daggers, clubs and axes, spears and polearms, bows and slings, siege engines, firearms, artillery and missiles, explosives, armour and shields, chariots and tanks, warships, aircraft and drones, and chemical, nuclear and cyber weapons — and the treaties that ban or limit some of them.

How to use Weapons Atlas

  1. Search, or pick a group such as Siege engines or Firearms, or an era.
  2. Open any card to read who invented it, how it worked, how it was used and a famous moment. Use ← Older and Newer → to walk through history.
  3. Switch to the timeline to see every era with a short introduction.
  4. Press “Generate quiz” and try to beat your best score, or download a printable quiz with an answer key.

What does Weapons Atlas cover?

160 weapons, armour, siege engines, warships and aircraft from the first stone tools 2.6 million years ago to the drones of today, each with who invented it, how it worked, how it was used and a famous moment in history.

Does it explain how to make weapons?

No. It is a history atlas, like a museum: it explains how each weapon worked in general terms and what it changed, never how to make or modify one.

Is Weapons Atlas free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account and no paywall. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
