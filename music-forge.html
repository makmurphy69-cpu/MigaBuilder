      🔑 How to get your own Gemini key (step by step)
      
A Gemini API key is a password-like code from Google that lets this page ask Google’s music AI (Lyria) for songs on your own account. The same key works in Picture Forge and in every MigaBuilder AI tool.

      Open Google AI Studio → Get API key ↗
      
    
        
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with any Google account (the one you use for Gmail or YouTube works).
    2. 
        
  2. Accept Google’s terms if it asks.
    3. 
        
  3. Press Create API key. If it asks for a project, pick the suggested one or press Create project.
    4. 
        
  4. Press Copy. The key is a long code that usually starts with AIza.
    5. 
        
  5. Back here, paste it in step 6 and press Test.
    6. 
        
  6. Turn on billing for music. Google’s music models have no free tier. In AI Studio open Usage & billing (or follow Google’s billing guide) and link a billing account to the key’s project. New Google Cloud accounts often get free trial credit.
    7. 
        
  7. Smart extra step: set a budget alert in Google Cloud Billing (for example 5 USD) so Google emails you before you spend more than planned.
    8. 
      

      
Is it safe? The key goes straight from your browser to Google, never to MigaBuilder. Unless you tick Remember, it is forgotten when you close the tab. Anyone with your key can spend on your account, so don’t share it. If it leaks, delete it on the same API keys page and create a new one.

    

    

      ⬇ How to download your song
      
    
        
  1. When the song is ready, press ⬇ Download MP3 for a small file that plays everywhere (phones, cars, WhatsApp, video editors).
    2. 
        
  2. Press ⬇ Download WAV for an uncompressed file, best for further editing in Audio Forge or a music program. It is about ten times bigger.
    3. 
        
  3. On a computer the file goes to your Downloads folder. On an iPhone or iPad tap Download in the pop-up and find it in the Files app. On Android it goes to Downloads.
    4. 
        
  4. 📋 Copy lyrics copies the words the AI sang, for a video description or a lyric sheet.
    5. 
        
  5. Earlier songs stay under Your recent songs in this browser; press ▶ to hear one again or ⬇ to download it.
    6. 
      

      
Google adds an inaudible SynthID watermark to every AI song so it can be recognised as AI-made. You can’t hear it.

    

    

      💾 Using a downloaded copy of MigaBuilder
      
    
        
  1. On the GitHub page press Code → Download ZIP and unzip it.
    2. 
        
  2. In that folder run python3 -m http.server 8000 and open http://localhost:8000/music-forge.html. (Double-clicking the file is not enough: the pages load shared files from the site root.)
    3. 
        
  3. AI songs work the same with your own key. The Quick presets and Tracker tabs need no key and work offline.
    4. 
      

    

    

      💡 Tips for great songs
      
    
        
  • Say what the song is about, where it plays and how it should feel: “a cosy rainy-day café song, soft piano and brushed drums”.
    • 
        
  • Short idea? Press ✨ Improve my words. Want to sing along? Choose My own words and press ✍️ Write them for me, then change any line.
    • 
        
  • Mark parts of your lyrics with [Verse], [Chorus] and [Bridge] so the AI knows what to repeat.
    • 
        
  • Try a 30-second clip first, then make the full song once you like the sound.
    • 
        
  • For video background music, pick No singing and the Steady background or Seamless loop shape.
    • 
      

    

    

      💰 Costs, rights and rules
      
With your own key Google charges your account about $0.04 per 30-second clip and $0.08 per full song (see Google’s price list; prices can change). Google refuses requests that copy a real artist’s voice or copyrighted lyrics; describe the style instead (“90s grunge”, not a band name). Check Google’s terms before using songs commercially. Music from the free Quick presets and Tracker tabs is yours with no conditions.
