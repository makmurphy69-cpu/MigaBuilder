About Post Forge

Post Forge lets you write one announcement and post it to your own Discord, Bluesky and Mastodon accounts at once.

How to use Post Forge

  1. Choose the platform and describe the message, audience, and goal.
  2. Press “Create post”.
  3. Refine the wording and image, then copy or publish the post.

Which social networks does it support?

Discord, Bluesky and Mastodon, using your own accounts. You can also copy the text for any other network.

Is Post Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

Your post goes only to the services you choose (Discord, Bluesky or Mastodon), sent from your browser with your own credentials. MigaBuilder does not post for you.
