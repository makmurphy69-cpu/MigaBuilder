Tips

🎯 Best results
Flat logos, icons and drawings with clear edges trace beautifully. Use the biggest, sharpest copy you have. Photos turn into a painted, poster-like look rather than an exact copy.
✂️ Clean files
Start with few colors, then add more only if parts are missing. Raise Remove specks if you see dust, and use Smooth first for blurry or compressed JPGs.
🔒 Private
Your image is never uploaded. Tracing runs in this page with the open-source ImageTracer library, so it also works offline once loaded.

About Vector Forge

Vector Forge turns a PNG or JPG logo, icon, drawing or signature into a sharp, scalable SVG vector.

How to use Vector Forge

  1. Open a PNG or JPG logo, icon or drawing, or press Try an example logo.
  2. Press “Download SVG”.
  3. Pick a preset, adjust colors and detail, remove the background if you like, then download the SVG.

How do I convert a PNG to SVG?

Open the PNG, pick a preset, adjust the colours and detail, remove the background if you like, and press Download SVG.

Is Vector Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
