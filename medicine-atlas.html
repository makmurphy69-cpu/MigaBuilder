Plagues and pandemics, and the vaccines, medicines, discoveries and public-health ideas that beat them — from ancient remedies to penicillin, mRNA vaccines and gene editing.

About Medicine & Disease Atlas

Medicine & Disease Atlas covers the great epidemics and pandemics of history, from the Plague of Athens and the Black Death to Spanish flu, HIV and COVID-19, and the people and ideas that fought them: vaccines from smallpox to mRNA, medicines from willow bark to antibiotics and insulin, scientific discoveries from Vesalius and germ theory to DNA and CRISPR, the tools doctors use to look inside the body, and public-health work such as clean water, sewers, nursing and the eradication of smallpox.

How to use Medicine & Disease Atlas

  1. Search a disease, a medicine or a scientist, or pick a group such as Vaccines or Pandemics.
  2. Open a card to read what it is, how it works or spreads, and what it changed.
  3. Use the timeline to follow medicine from ancient times to today.
  4. Play the quiz, or download a printable quiz with answers.

Is the Medicine & Disease Atlas medical advice?

No. It is a history of medicine for learning. For your own health, ask a doctor, nurse or pharmacist.

What does it cover?

Pandemics and diseases, vaccines, medicines, scientific discoveries, tools for diagnosis and public-health milestones, each with who was involved, how it works and what it changed.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account and no paywall. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
