Flashcard Forge

Make flashcards in seconds, then remember them for good with spaced repetition, games and tests.

Your decks

🔒 Decks are saved only in this browser (IndexedDB) — nothing is uploaded. Export a backup or share a link to move a deck to another device.

Shortcuts: Space flip · 1–4 grade · U undo · S star · R read aloud · E edit

Tip: write {{word}} in the front to make a fill-in-the-blank card. Use **bold** and *italic*. Tags are separated by commas.

Paste many cards at once

One card per line: front - back, front ; back, front,back or tab-separated (e.g. copied from a spreadsheet, Quizlet or Anki). An optional third column is used as tags.

Paste your notes, a textbook paragraph or a word list — or just type a topic — and the AI writes cards. You can also load a text file.

Flashcard Forge · MigaBuilder · Scheduling based on the SM-2 spaced-repetition algorithm (the family used by Anki). Everything stays in your browser.
