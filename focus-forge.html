    How to focus better

    🍅 How the Pomodoro method works
    1. Pick one task.
    2. Focus for one session (25 minutes) — no email, no phone.
    3. Take a 5-minute break: stand up, look away from the screen.
    4. After four sessions, take a longer break of 15–30 minutes.

    If a thought pops up, write it in the task list and get back to work.

    ⏱️ Which length to pick

    25/5 is a good start and suits tasks you avoid. 50/10 suits deep work like writing or coding once you are in the flow. 90/20 matches a long deep-work block; use it once or twice a day. Shorter sessions (15/3) help on tired days or with young learners.

    📵 Protect the session

    Put your phone in another room or face down, close tabs you don't need, and tell people when you'll be free. Keep a notepad for “later” thoughts instead of acting on them.

    ☕ Use breaks well

    Move, drink water, look out of a window. Skip social media: it makes it harder to start again. After a long break, first reread your task list.

    📚 Studying?

    Spend focus sessions testing yourself rather than rereading — try Flashcard Forge and the techniques in Memory Forge. Use a break to write down what you remember from the last session.

    📈 Estimate and learn

    Guess how many sessions each task needs. Comparing guesses with the real count, week after week, makes your plans more honest.
