Media Convert Forge

Trim, rotate, resize and compress video, or turn it into a GIF or MP3 — all inside your browser.

🔒 Processing happens locally with FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly. Your video never leaves this tab. The engine (~25 MB) downloads on first use; large videos may be slow on phones.

GIFs are capped at 20 seconds and 640 px wide so they stay a sensible size.

Part of MigaBuilder.
