About Invoice Forge

Invoice Forge makes clean quotes, invoices and receipts. Enter your business, your client and the line items, and it adds up the discount, tax, shipping and total in your currency. It numbers each document, adds the date and the due date, shows how to pay you, and downloads a PDF.

How to use Invoice Forge

  1. Enter your business, the client and the line items. Choose the currency, the number, the date and when payment is due.
  2. Add how to pay you and any notes.
  3. Check the live preview and press “Download PDF”, print it, or download it as HTML. The next number is suggested for you next time.

Can I save the invoice as a PDF?

Yes. Press Download PDF. The text in the PDF can be selected and searched. You can also print it or download it as an HTML file.

Is Invoice Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
