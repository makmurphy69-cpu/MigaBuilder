About Everyday Forge

Everyday Forge collects everyday helpers: charts, writing improvements, password generation, unit conversion, current currency rates, and money calculators for loans and mortgages, savings with compound interest, price, margin and VAT, and percentages.

How to use Everyday Forge

  1. Choose the everyday calculator or helper you need and enter its details.
  2. Press “Calculate”.
  3. Review the result and copy or use it.

Are the generated passwords private?

Yes. Passwords are generated in your browser and never sent anywhere.

How is the loan payment calculated?

It uses the standard annuity formula: the same payment every month, with more of it going to interest at the start and more to the loan at the end. Add an extra monthly payment to see how much sooner the loan is paid off and how much interest you save.

Is Everyday Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
