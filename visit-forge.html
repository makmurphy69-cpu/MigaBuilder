About Visit Forge

Visit Forge helps you get the most out of a short doctor's appointment. Write down the main reason you are going, each symptom (when it started, how often, how bad, what makes it better or worse), a timeline, your medicines and allergies, and your questions. It turns them into a 30-second opening to say first and a clear one-page summary to print or hand over, with your three most important questions at the top. After the visit, write down what the doctor said and the plan.

How to use Visit Forge

  1. Start with the one thing you most want help with.
  2. Add your symptoms, medicines and questions, and star your top three questions.
  3. Download or print the one-page summary, read the opening out first, and fill in “After the visit” on your way out.

Tips for a short appointment

  • Say your main worry first, even if it feels embarrassing.
  • Bring your medicines, or a photo of their boxes.
  • Ask “What should I watch for, and when should I come back?” before you leave.
  • Take someone with you if you can. A second pair of ears helps.

Is my health information uploaded?

No. Visit Forge has no AI and no server. Everything you type stays in this browser, and the PDF is made on your device.

Does Visit Forge give medical advice?

No. It helps you describe what is happening and ask good questions. Only a doctor or nurse can tell you what it is and what to do. If you think it is an emergency, call your local emergency number now.

Is Visit Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.
