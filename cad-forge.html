Shortcuts
  • Wall tool: click to start, click again for each corner. Type a length and press Enter for an exact wall.
  • Click the first corner to close the outline. Press Esc or right-click to stop.
  • Hold Shift to switch ortho lock.
  • Drag a corner to move it. Drag a wall to move it and stretch the walls joined to it.
  • Scroll to zoom. Drag empty space to pan.
  • Delete removes the selected item.

About CAD Forge

CAD Forge turns a floor plan into a 3D house: draw walls, doors and windows, and it builds the roof and timber framing, plan and elevation drawings and a material list.

How to use CAD Forge

  1. Set the wall height, wall thickness and roof, then draw walls, doors and windows on the plan.
  2. Press “Load sample house”.
  3. Check the 3D house and timber frame, then download the drawings, material list and DXF file.

Which files can I download?

Plan and elevation drawings, a material list, and DXF, SVG, OBJ and STL files.

Is CAD Forge free?

Yes. CAD Forge is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. CAD Forge runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
