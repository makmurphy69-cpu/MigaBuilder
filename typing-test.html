Audio transcription test

For transcriptionist, captioning, medical scribe, legal and dispatcher jobs. Listen and type what you hear. Use Ctrl + Space to pause or play, and Ctrl + ← to hear the last sentence again. The voice is your device's own speech voice.

Clerical checking test

Common in government, civil service, bank, post office and admin tests. Compare each pair: are they exactly the same or different? Press S or D on the keyboard, or tap the buttons. Score = right answers minus wrong answers.

Proofreading test

For admin, legal, editing, marketing and customer service jobs. Click every word with a mistake: spelling, wrong word (their/there), repeated words, grammar or punctuation. Click again to unmark it. Then press Check.

What speed do jobs ask for?

Typical requirements seen in job postings and pre-employment tests. They vary by employer and country, so always check the job posting. The last column compares your best results saved in this browser.

Your best results

Tips for the real test: warm up for 5 minutes first, use a full-size keyboard with a number pad for 10-key, sit straight with your wrists floating, and read the instructions on how to move between fields (Tab or Enter). Accuracy usually matters more than speed: many tests reject results under 95–98%.
