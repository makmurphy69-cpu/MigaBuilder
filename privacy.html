Privacy policy

What stays on your device, what is sent online, and to whom.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The short version

  • No accounts. You never sign up, so we have no profile, password or email address for you.
  • Your files stay on your device in tools marked 🔒 Runs in your browser. PDFs, photos, videos and documents are processed by your own browser and are never uploaded to us.
  • No advertising cookies and no tracking across sites. We count visits without cookies.
  • Some tools need an online service to work (AI steps, video rooms, screen sharing). Each one is labelled on the homepage and on the tool page, and only what that step needs is sent.

Who runs this site

MigaBuilder (migabuilder.com) is an independent, open-source project. For anything about your privacy, write to contact@migabuilder.com.

What is stored in your browser

Tools save things like your recent and favourite tools, your language, drafts, settings and the work you choose to keep (“My work”) in your browser’s own storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). This stays on your device; we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. If you paste your own AI key into a tool, it is kept only in memory unless you tick “Remember”, in which case it is stored in this browser only.

AI services

When you use the free Gemini option, your request goes through MigaBuilder’s proxy to Google’s Gemini API. The proxy does not store your text. Google processes it under its own terms, and on the free tier Google may use it to improve its services, so don’t send anything private or confidential to an AI step. If you choose your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini key, your browser talks to that company directly and their privacy policy applies.

Children

MigaBuilder has no accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Teachers and parents should supervise the tools that use AI or go online.

Your rights

Because we do not keep accounts or profiles, there is usually nothing about you for us to show, correct or delete. If you think we hold something (for example a shop order or a feedback message), write to contact@migabuilder.com and we will help. If you are in the EU, UK or another place with data-protection law, you can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If what the site sends changes, we update this page and the date at the top. The site is open source, so you can check exactly what each page does on GitHub.
