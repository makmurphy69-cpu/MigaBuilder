Invention Atlas

The inventions that changed the world, from fire and the wheel to smartphones and AI: who invented each one, how it works and what it changed.

About Invention Atlas

Invention Atlas tells the stories of inventions in tools and making, farming and food, energy and power, transport, communication, computing, science instruments, everyday life, materials and building. Each entry explains who invented it, how it works in plain words, what it changed, and a surprising fact — with credit to inventors from every part of the world.

How to use Invention Atlas

  1. Search an invention or an inventor, or pick a group such as Transport or Computing.
  2. Open a card to read who invented it, how it works and what it changed.
  3. Use the timeline to see what was invented in each era.
  4. Play the quiz, or download a printable quiz with answers.

What does Invention Atlas cover?

Inventions in tools, farming, energy, transport, communication, computing, science, everyday life, materials and building — from stone tools to AI — each with who invented it, how it works and what it changed.

Why do some inventions have several inventors?

Many inventions were made by several people at about the same time, or improved step by step. The atlas names the main people and explains who did what.

Is Invention Atlas free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account and no paywall. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
