    About PDF Forge

    PDF Forge merges, splits, reorders, rotates, numbers and watermarks PDFs, and creates PDFs from images.

    How to use PDF Forge

    1. Choose one or more PDF files and set the page order or rotation.
    2. Press “Merge / create PDF”.
    3. Download the finished PDF or split pages into a ZIP.

    How do I merge PDF files?

    Choose all the PDFs, drag the pages into the order you want, and press Merge / create PDF to download one file.

    Is PDF Forge free?

    Yes. PDF Forge is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. PDF Forge runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
