How to play

WASD or arrow keys to move and turn · Space to jump (in the arena: zap) · drag with the mouse to look around · on a phone use the on-screen buttons. Runner: ← → change lane, Space jumps. Racer: ↑ accelerate, ↓ brake, ← → steer.

How it works

The AI does not write thousands of lines of code that might break. It writes a short game description — template, world, hero, enemies, speeds and goal — and a built-in 3D engine (three.js) turns it into a game that always runs. You can change everything afterwards with the menus, sliders and level painter. The share link holds the whole game inside it, and the download is one HTML file that works on any website or offline (it loads three.js from a CDN).

🔒 Games are made and played in your browser. Only your description is sent to the AI you choose.
