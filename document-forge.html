About Document Forge

Document Forge converts DOCX, text, Markdown, HTML, CSV, images and PDF files.

How to use Document Forge

  1. Drop in a document, image, or PDF and choose the output format.
  2. Press “Convert & download”.
  3. Check the preview and open the converted download.

Which formats can I convert?

DOCX, plain text, Markdown, HTML, CSV, images and PDF. Choose the output format and download the converted file.

Is Document Forge free?

Yes. Document Forge is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Document Forge runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
