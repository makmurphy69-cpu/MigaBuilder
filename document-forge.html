About Document Forge

Document Forge converts PDF to Word, and DOCX, text, Markdown, HTML, CSV, images and PDF files.

How to use Document Forge

  1. Drop in a document, image, or PDF and choose the output format.
  2. Press “Convert & download”.
  3. Check the preview and open the converted download.

Which formats can I convert?

DOCX, plain text, Markdown, HTML, CSV, images and PDF. Choose the output format and download the converted file.

Can I convert a PDF to Word?

Yes. Choose “Word document (.docx)” after adding a PDF. The text, headings, paragraphs, bold and italics become editable in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice. Pictures and table lines are not copied, and a scanned PDF without a text layer is added as page pictures — use OCR Forge first to turn a scan into text.

Is Document Forge free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. It runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
