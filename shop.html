No. Load the zip into the free MigaBuilder Website Builder, fill in your details once, click any text to change it, and download your finished site. Prefer code? Any text editor works too. The guide and the video above show each click.

About an hour the first time: 10 minutes for your details, 20–30 minutes to write your own texts and prices, and 10–20 minutes to publish. Watch the video first to see if that suits you.

Stripe is the seller of record. It handles payment, sales tax and VAT, and sends your receipt.
Can I use it for clients?
Yes, on as many of your own or your clients' websites as you like. You may not resell or give away the templates themselves.

Payment runs on Stripe's checkout, a separate website from MigaBuilder. Stripe (as seller of record) and Link process your name, email, address country and card under their own privacy policies.
