About Coloring Forge

Coloring Forge makes printable pages for children (and adults): coloring pages from a library of more than 3,000 pictures, from a description or a photo, mandalas, alphabet pages, letter and name tracing, Chinese character practice with stroke order, paint by numbers and mazes. Collect them into a book with a cover and print it or save it as a PDF.

How to use Coloring Forge

  1. Type your child’s name and pick the paper size.
  2. Pick a picture from the library (search for “dinosaur” or “fire truck”), or choose another page type and press the button.
  3. Press “Add to book” for every page you like, then download the book as a PDF or print it.

What can I make with Coloring Forge?

Coloring pages from a free library of more than 3,000 pictures (animals, vehicles, food, fairy tales, flags with a colour guide and more), from a description (made by AI) or from your own photo, mandalas, alphabet pages, letter and name tracing worksheets, Chinese character practice sheets with stroke order (plus Japanese, Korean, Arabic and other alphabets), paint by numbers from a photo, mazes and a cover with your child's name.

How do I learn to write Chinese characters with it?

Pick a set of characters, such as numbers or family words. Each character gets a big model with pinyin and meaning, every stroke in the right order, grey copies to trace in a 米 grid and empty boxes to practise. Use Alphabet Forge to hear each character and watch it being written.

Do I need AI to get a coloring page?

No. The picture library has more than 3,000 ready pictures in 16 groups — animals, vehicles, food, fairy tales, people and jobs, sports, flags of the world and more. Search for a word, tap a picture and print. Flags come with a small coloured guide in the corner. Hand-drawn pictures are from OpenMoji (CC BY-SA 4.0); bold pictures are drawn with Google’s Noto Emoji font.

How does paint by numbers work?

Choose a photo and the number of colours. Coloring Forge finds the main colours, splits the picture into areas big enough to paint and numbers them. The colour key tells you how to mix every colour from red, yellow, blue, white and black.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photo coloring pages and paint by numbers are made in your browser. Only the words you type for an AI coloring page are sent to Google Gemini.
