Policy Forge

Find out what your website or app must tell its visitors, and generate the privacy policy, cookie policy, consent banner, terms and disclosures — based on what is actually on your site. Free and private.

2. Scan your site's code

Open your website, press Ctrl+U (⌘+Option+U on a Mac) to see its code, copy everything and paste it here. Do this for a few pages, such as the home page, contact page and checkout. Nothing is uploaded.

3. What your site uses and does

Tick everything that applies. The scan ticks what it finds; add what it cannot see, such as your newsletter tool or a payment provider used on another page.

4. Your documents

Policy Forge writes clear, plain-language drafts based on common requirements of the GDPR, UK GDPR, the ePrivacy rules on cookies, California's CCPA/CPRA and similar laws. It is not legal advice. Laws change and every business is different — check the result against what you really do, and ask a lawyer if you handle sensitive data, children's data or large amounts of personal data.
