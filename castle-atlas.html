Castle Atlas

Walls, castles and forts from the first town walls to Cold War bunkers: how each one defended, the parts of a castle, and the sieges that tested them.

About Castle Atlas

Castle Atlas covers fortifications on every continent: early town walls and hillforts, ancient walls from Mycenae and Babylon to the Great Wall of China and Hadrian’s Wall, medieval castles from motte-and-bailey to concentric castles, fortresses in Africa, Asia and the Americas, the parts of a castle such as moats, gatehouses, portcullises and murder holes, siege tactics, gunpowder-age star forts and modern lines and bunkers.

How to use Castle Atlas

  1. Search a castle, a country or a castle part, or pick a group such as Medieval castles or Castle parts.
  2. Open a card to read how it defended and a famous siege.
  3. Use the timeline to see how walls changed with each new weapon.
  4. Play the quiz, or download a printable quiz with answers.

What does Castle Atlas cover?

Town walls, hillforts, ancient walls, medieval castles, fortresses around the world, the parts of a castle, siege tactics, star forts and modern fortifications — each with how it defended and a famous siege or story.

Can I see castles from outside Europe?

Yes. It includes fortresses and walls from Africa, Asia, the Middle East and the Americas, such as Great Zimbabwe, Benin City’s walls, Sacsayhuamán, Himeji Castle and Hwaseong Fortress.

Is Castle Atlas free?

Yes. It is free to use, with no account and no paywall. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
