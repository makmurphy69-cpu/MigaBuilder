About Image Studio

Image Studio removes or replaces image backgrounds with on-device AI, builds collages, memes and YouTube thumbnails, and upscales photos.

How to use Image Studio

  1. Choose one or more images and select the background, collage, meme, thumbnail, or upscale tab.
  2. Press “Apply background effect”.
  3. Check the live canvas and download the finished PNG.

How do I remove a background?

Open the image in the background tab and apply the effect. The AI model runs on your device, so the photo is not uploaded.

Is Image Studio free?

Yes. Image Studio is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Image Studio runs entirely in your browser, so your files and text stay on your device.
