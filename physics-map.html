    About Physics Map

    Physics Map is a free, visual way to explore physics in your browser — for school, for curiosity, or to check a number. There is nothing to install and no account.

    How to use it

    1. Pick a view: the Solar System, the history of the universe, black holes or the theory map.
    2. Tap anything to open its facts, its numbers and a “did you know”. Download a fact sheet or ask the AI to explain it simply.
    3. Use the calculators to work out your weight on other planets, light travel times, orbits, E = mc², time dilation or the size of a black hole.
    4. Finish with a quiz, or print one with an answer key for a class.

    Questions

    How much would I weigh on Mars? About 38% of your Earth weight: a person who weighs 60 kg on Earth would read about 23 kg on a scale on Mars.

    How big is a black hole? Its event horizon grows with its mass: about 3 km across for every Sun of mass it holds (radius 2.95 km per solar mass).

    How old is the universe? About 13.8 billion years, from measurements of the cosmic microwave background.

    Is the data accurate? Planet data follows NASA’s planetary fact sheets, rounded. Speculative ideas such as string theory and the multiverse are marked as untested.
