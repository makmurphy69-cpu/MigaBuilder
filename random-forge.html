      About Random Forge

      Random Forge is a free random idea generator that is connected to real tools. Press one button for a random game, picture, idea, website, drawing prompt or story, keep the parts you like, and open it in the MigaBuilder tool that builds it.

      How to use Random Forge

      1. Pick what to make, or leave it on Surprise me, and choose a topic if you want one.
      2. Press “Create something random”. Lock the parts you like and reroll the rest.
      3. Press Build it to open the idea in the matching tool, or share, save or download it.

      What can Random Forge make?

      A random game, picture, idea, website, drawing prompt or story. Press Build it to open the idea in the matching MigaBuilder tool: 3D Game Forge, Picture Forge, Mind Map, Website Builder, Paint Forge or 3D Cartoon.

      Can I keep the parts I like?

      Yes. Press the lock next to any part and reroll the rest, or tap one part to reroll only that one.

      Is it a random game idea generator?

      Yes, and more: a random game becomes a playable 3D game in one click, with its world, hero, enemies and twist already set. It is also a random drawing prompt, story prompt, picture prompt and business or website idea generator.

      Is Random Forge free?

      Yes. It is free to use, with no account, no watermark and no paywall.

      Are my ideas uploaded?

      Rolling, locking, sharing and saving favourites all happen in your browser. Only when you press a button with AI is the idea sent to Google's Gemini model through MigaBuilder's free AI service, or to the AI you choose.
